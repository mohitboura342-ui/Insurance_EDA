# 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on an **Insurance Charges Dataset** to understand the factors influencing medical insurance costs.

The notebook explores customer demographics, lifestyle habits, health indicators, and their relationship with insurance charges using statistical analysis and visualizations.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

> **Which type of customer usually costs the insurance company the most?**

The analysis investigates whether insurance charges are influenced by:

- 🚬 Smoking
- 👴 Age
- ⚖️ BMI
- 👨 Gender
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Size
- 🌍 Region

---

# 💡 Initial Hypothesis

✔ Smokers pay significantly higher insurance charges.

✔ Older individuals generally have higher medical expenses.

✔ Higher BMI may increase insurance costs.

✔ Family size might slightly affect medical expenses.

✔ Region may have only a minor effect.

# 📈 Exploratory Data Analysis

## 🔹 Univariate Analysis

- Charges Distribution
- Children Distribution

### Insights

✅ BMI mostly lies in the healthy to overweight range.

---

## 🔹 Categorical Analysis

- Gender Distribution
- Smoker Distribution
- Region Distribution

### Insights

✅ Male and Female customers are nearly equal.

✅ Majority are non-smokers.

✅ Regions have balanced representation.

---

# 📊 Bivariate Analysis

## Age vs Charges

✔ Insurance charges increase with age.

Older individuals generally require more healthcare services.

---

## BMI vs Charges

Higher BMI tends to increase insurance costs.

---

🚨 Smoking is the strongest predictor of insurance cost.

Smokers pay dramatically higher insurance charges than non-smokers.

---

## Children vs Charges

Family size has only a slight impact on insurance expenses.
